Paste a batch. Queue a batch.

Fast Scheduler reads your message block by block: the first line is when, everything below it is what gets published. Five blocks in one message create five scheduled posts — and you can send another batch whenever you like.

  • Any date you already write — today 10:23, tomorrow 7:24 PM, 12.12.2026 18:28, 23-10-2027 15:34, 2026-12-12 18:28 or a bare 18:28.
  • Buttons per post — <inline>Text<link="https://example.com">, with -- for one row and // for a new row.
  • Too long for one message? Save the same text as a .txt file and upload it while scheduling — one blank line between posts.
  • Free and Premium — batches are not paywalled. On Free the only ceiling is the 100-message pending queue.

Questions, answered

Is Fast Scheduler really free?

Yes. The free plan connects 1 channel and 1 sender bot with up to 100 pending scheduled messages and 100 sends per channel per month. Premium raises limits and unlocks leaderboards and custom reports — but you can run a small channel on Free forever.

How does it save me time?

Batch scheduling turns "post every day" into one weekly 10-minute session. Paste several date + message blocks in a single message — a blank line between posts — and every block becomes its own scheduled post. The same text can be uploaded as a .txt file, and batches work on Free and Premium. Recurring posts make daily digests fully automatic, and reports come to you instead of you hunting analytics.

Do posts come from my own bot?

Yes — create a bot in @BotFather, connect it once, and every scheduled post is published through it, keeping your brand on every message. The sender bot is required: scheduling unlocks only after your channel has one (the main bot steps in only as an emergency fallback if your bot's token fails).

Will posts go out if I'm offline or asleep?

Yes. Scheduling happens on the bot's side: once a message is queued it publishes at the exact time you chose, in your timezone, whether you're online or not.

Can I see who comments on my posts?

Premium leaderboards rank your top posts by views, comments and reactions, plus a top-commentators ranking. Add the bot to your channel's discussion group and it attributes comment authors automatically.

Do I have to make @FastSchedulerBot a channel admin?

No. Publishing, scheduling, statistics and admin management all run through your own sender bot. The main bot is your chat for support and the optional emergency stand-in — if your sender bot's token is revoked it can deliver one post and keep going for up to 3 days, but only after you say yes. Existing channels never need to be reconnected.

Can other people help me run the channel?

Yes. Co-admins are real channel administrators: they send the sender bot's @username (or its token) once, the bot verifies their rights through that bot, and they get 15 granular permissions — scheduling, editing, deleting, statistics, export and more. The owner is notified, a channel marked "hide from strangers" is never named to them, requests are limited to one per permission per week, and every change is audited.

Is my data safe?

Bot tokens are encrypted at rest, backups support AES-256-GCM password encryption, and you can export or delete everything at any time. See our Privacy Policy.
