Help Center Scheduling Messages

Scheduling Messages

37 articles

A scheduled message is a post that waits for its moment. You create it once; the bot publishes it at the chosen time in the channel's time zone, even when your phone is offline.

The basic flow in six steps

1. In the sender bot for your channel, tap 📅 Schedule a Message (or send /schedule).
2. Send the date/time on the first line and the post below it.
3. Choose how media is attached: 📷 Normal, 🎲 Random, 🔀 Sequential, ⏭ Skip Photos or 💾From storage.
4. Send the media files, then tap ✅ Done (N).
5. Check the Review card — channel, time, preview, media and button counts.
6. Tap ✅ Schedule. The post is queued and gets an ID.

What you can schedule

Text, photos, videos, animations, documents, voice and audio, locations, stickers, polls and quizzes, and albums of up to 10 items. Formatting, links, spoilers and inline buttons survive the trip. The exact support matrix is in Supported Content Types.

What you cannot do

  • Premium/custom emoji are stripped (the text stays).
  • Telegram date formatting and rich Articles are not rendered.
  • Checklists are unsupported.
  • Polls must be anonymous — that is a Telegram rule for channels.
  • Scheduling needs a connected channel with a sender bot. Without one the button explains what is missing.

Managing the queue

Everything you queue appears in 📋 Message List and 📅 Calendar. From there you can preview, edit, delete, pause a recurring rule, or find its ID. The list is the source of truth — a post exists until it is sent or deleted.

Help Center Channels

Channels

15 articles

A channel is the destination. Everything else in Fast Scheduler exists to publish into it reliably.

What a connected channel means

Three conditions are true: you are an administrator, @FastSchedulerBot is an administrator with Post Messages, and the channel is linked to your account. Then it appears in 📢 Channels with its own time zone and sender bot.

Channel vs sender bot

The channel is where readers are; the sender bot is the author that publishes there. One sender bot per channel. Fast Scheduler connects the two, which is why both must be configured before scheduling works.

What you can configure per channel

  • Time zone — what "19:00" means for this channel.
  • Sender bot — the bot that publishes.
  • Signature — an optional footer (Premium).
  • Admins — co-managers with limited rights (inside the sender bot).
  • Queue — scheduled and recurring messages, visible in the list and calendar.

Limits

Free: 1 channel. Premium: up to 3. Each extra channel needs its own sender bot.

Help Center Sender Bots

Sender Bots

13 articles

A sender bot is a Telegram bot you own, connected to exactly one channel, that publishes your posts under its own name.

Why it is required

Telegram needs a bot with Post Messages rights in the channel. Using your own bot also gives your channel a stable author identity, lower sending delays and per-channel statistics.

The lifecycle

1. Create it in @BotFather.
2. Connect its token in 🤖 Bots.
3. Assign it to a channel.
4. Promote it in that channel with Post Messages.
5. Manage it later: change token, replace, switch channel, swap, remove.

Limits

Free: 1 bot. Premium: up to 3. One bot per channel.

Start here

  • Add a Sender Bot — creation and connection.
  • Manage Sender Bots — the Manage menu.
  • Sender Bot Management — what moves inside the bot.
  • Bot Permissions and Bot Status — rights and health.
  • Rotate Token — after a leak or a reset.
  • Remove a Sender Bot — detach without losing posts.

Never share a token: anyone holding it can post as your bot.

Help Center Admins

Admins

4 articles

Admins are other Telegram accounts that can work on your channel without owning it.

Where it lives

Inside the sender bot for that channel: ⚙️ Settings → 👥 Admins. Only the channel's Main Owner can change permissions or transfer ownership.

What you can control

Scheduling, recurring messages, message list, editing, deleting, statistics, export, settings and bot management — each grantable separately.

How it works

The owner opens an admin's profile, toggles permissions, reviews a summary of changes and saves. The admin's menu updates immediately; disabled features disappear from their buttons.

Requests

An admin who lacks a permission can request it from the bot. The owner gets the request with an optional message and can grant or deny it.

Ownership transfer

Protected by a typed confirmation phrase, a final confirm button and acceptance by the target, with a 24-hour expiry. The old owner stays as a regular admin.

Limits are shared

All admins of one channel share that channel's limits: scheduled cap, monthly sends, recurring rules. See How Admins Share Limits.

Help Center Premium

Premium

33 articles

Premium is the paid plan. It lifts the caps that stop a growing channel: more channels, more bots, unlimited queues, bigger files, storage, signatures and full statistics.

What it changes

  • 3 channels and 3 sender bots instead of 1/1.
  • Unlimited scheduled, recurring and sent messages.
  • Uploads up to 100 MB; video up to up to 50MB; 4K allowed.
  • 20 storage boxes with 500 items and stored video.
  • Signatures, leaderboards, multi-channel targeting, protected backups.

How payment works

Premium is paid with Telegram Stars — in-app, instant, no card details. Monthly auto-renews; 3-month, 6-month and yearly are one-time purchases.

What happens when it ends

Nothing is deleted on day one. A grace period lets you choose what to keep; later stages pause, then disconnect excess assets. Full timeline: Premium Expiry.

Referrals

14 articles

The referral program rewards you for bringing other channel owners to Fast Scheduler.

The deal

Both sides earn 3 Premium days when an invitee connects a channel and creates at least one message. Up to 15 qualifying referrals — 45 days total.

Your link

How days work

They are banked, not applied automatically. Activate them when you need Premium; if you already pay, they wait until the paid period ends.

Help Center Limits

Limits

16 articles

Plans differ in five numbers: pending scheduled messages, sends per month, recurring messages, channels and sender bots. Storage and media size add a second group.

Free

  • 100 pending scheduled messages per channel
  • 100 sent messages per channel per month
  • 1 recurring message
  • 1 channel, 1 sender bot
  • 1 storage box with 100 items
  • uploads up to 10 MB, video up to up to 25MB

Premium

  • unlimited pending and sent messages
  • unlimited recurring messages
  • 3 channels, 3 sender bots
  • 20 boxes with 500 items
  • uploads up to 100 MB, video up to up to 50MB

How counting works

Limits are per channel and shared by all admins of that channel. The monthly counter counts messages actually sent and resets on the 1st. `/stats` shows current usage.

Fixed limits

Some things cannot be raised by any plan: one sender bot per channel, anonymous polls only in channels (Telegram's rule), and the maximum album size of 10 items.

Help Center Time Zone

Time Zone

8 articles

Time zones decide what the numbers in your schedule mean. Get this right once and scheduling becomes predictable.

Two levels

  • Account zone — the default, set in ⚙️ Settings → 🕐 Time Zone or with /timezone.
  • Channel zone — optional per channel, set on the channel card. It overrides the account zone for that channel.

How a date is read

A scheduled 19:00 is interpreted in the channel's zone. If the channel has no own zone, the account zone applies.

Detection

During the tutorial and in the time zone screen you can send a city, your current time, or a location. The bot proposes a zone and you confirm. If detection fails, pick from the list.

Language

6 articles

Language changes the bot's interface, not your content.

Available

  • English
  • Russian
  • Spanish

English is the fallback when a translation is missing.

Where to change it

🌐 Language in the menu, or /language. The change is immediate and stored per Telegram account, so it follows you across devices.

Sync

Fast Scheduler, the support bot and @SetDate_bot share your preference. When you change it in one, the others offer to sync.

What it does not do

  • Your channel posts are never translated.
  • Commands such as /schedule work in any language.
  • Dates and times still follow the selected time zone, not the language.

How do I disable language sync?

Go to Language on the main menu → tap Disable Language Sync. This stops cross-bot sync offers between Fast Scheduler and @SetDate_bot, and turns off Telegram language detection. You can re-enable it anytime from the same menu.

How do I hide language sync notifications?

Premium feature. Go to Language on the main menu → tap Hide Notifications. Sync still works but you won't see the notification popups. Toggle it back to show notifications again.

Help Center Backup, Export & Import

Backup, Export & Import

30 articles

Everything you scheduled lives on the bot's server, not in your phone. Export gives you a readable file of it, Backup gives you one file that can rebuild the account, and Import puts a file back in.

What each one is for
• Export — a copy you can read, print, or edit in a spreadsheet. Cannot rebuild the account by itself.
• Backup — a complete snapshot (.fsback / .fspback) that can be restored with Overwrite or Add.
• Import — takes a backup, a message list, a spreadsheet, or a SetDate schedule and turns it into scheduled messages.

Where it is
Main menu → Data. The screen has two buttons: Export and Import. Commands /export, /backup and /import open the same screens.

Do this before big changes
Take a backup before a mass delete, a channel move, or a long vacation. See Create a Backup and Importing Your Data.

The four management tools
• Message List — browse per channel, preview, edit, delete.
• Search — find a post by keyword.
• Calendar — the month at a glance.
• Statistics — counts, sends and per-channel numbers.

Common questions

How do I create a storage box and save media?

Step by step:
1. Click Media Storage on the main menu (page 2) or use /storage
2. Click Create Box
3. Enter a name for your box (e.g., "Product Photos")
4. The box is created — now click on it to open it
5. Send photos, videos, or files to the bot — they'll be saved in this box
6. Later, when scheduling a message, choose From Storage as the media option and pick from your collection

Free plan: 1 box, max 100 items, no videos. Premium: up to 20 boxes, 500 items.

How do I rename or delete a storage box?

To rename: click Media Storage → select the box → click Rename → type the new name → confirm.

To delete: click Media Storage → select the box → click Delete Box → confirm deletion. Warning: Deleting a box removes ALL media inside it permanently.

Can I move items between storage boxes?

Yes — bulk move and copy is a Premium feature. Step by step:
1. Click Media Storage → open the source box
2. Use Move All or Copy All (optionally filtered by type: images, videos, files)
3. Choose the destination box from the list
4. The items are moved/copied — no need to re-upload

On the Free plan you can move or copy one file at a time instead — open the item and use Move / Copy.

This helps you reorganize your media collection without re-uploading anything.

Can I download a whole storage box?

Yes — box download is a Premium feature: open the box and tap Download Box to get its files as an archive.

On the Free plan open any item and tap Download to save files one at a time, without compression.

What happens to my storage when Premium expires?

On the first day after expiry, you'll get a prompt to choose 1 storage box to keep. If you don't choose, the newest box is kept by default. Excess boxes are deleted after 17 days. The kept box is trimmed to the newest 100 items (videos are removed first, oldest first). To avoid this: reactivate Premium with /premium before day 17.

Can I store videos in Media Storage?

Free plan: No in storage — you'll get an error if you try. Premium: up to 50MB per video. Videos larger than the premium limit cannot be saved to storage even on Premium. To check your plan: use /premium or click Premium on the main menu.

Limits
How do I delete individual items from storage?

Step by step:
1. Click Media Storage on the main menu (or use /storage)
2. Select the storage box containing the items
3. Browse the list of media items
4. Click on an item → click Delete
5. Confirm deletion — this is permanent and cannot be undone

To delete everything: use Delete Box to remove the entire box and all contents.

Can I preview media before selecting it for a message?

Yes! When scheduling a message and you reach the media step:
1. Choose From Storage
2. Select a storage box
3. Each item shows a preview — you can see the image/video before picking it
4. Click on the one you want → it's attached to your scheduled message
5. Confirm and your message is ready with the selected media

Troubleshooting

15 articles

Most problems fall into five buckets:
1. Nothing was sent — the message is not there, or the sender bot lost admin rights — No Post Sent.
2. It was sent late or early — almost always a time zone difference — Late Post.
3. Media failed — the file was deleted, replaced or too large — Media Fails.
4. Permission errors — the bot is not an admin, or your role lacks the right — Permission.
5. The bot itself is unreachable — Bot Down.

Each article ends with what to send support if the fix does not work.

Common questions

How do I exit a stuck flow?

Use the /cancel command at any time to exit the current operation and return to the main menu.

Payment went through but Premium didn't activate

Wait 2-3 minutes — there can be a small delay with some payment providers. If still not active after 5 minutes, check with /premium. If Premium still shows as inactive, contact @MaximalXP with your user ID, payment method, and transaction details.

I used a referral link but didn't get Premium days

Referral Premium days are only credited when the referred person 1) is a NEW user (never used the bot before), and 2) connects a channel. Just starting the bot with the link is not enough. Check /referral to see your status. Contact @MaximalXP if requirements were met.

My scheduled messages stopped sending

Check: 1) Your sender bot is still connected and is admin in the channel. 2) You haven't hit the Free plan monthly send cap (100 per channel). 3) The channel hasn't been removed. 4) Your Premium hasn't expired (if you had channels above the free limit). Use /stats to check your status.

Premium ExpiryFrozen Assets
I accidentally deleted something — can I recover it?

If you have a backup (.fsback or .fspback file), you can restore it with /import. Without a backup, deleted data cannot be recovered. We recommend regular exports to avoid data loss.

How do I delete my account or all my data?

Contact @MaximalXP directly to request account deletion. You can also manually delete your data using the export/backup feature first to keep a copy. Your scheduled messages, media, and settings will be permanently removed.

Can I get a refund for Premium?

Each Stars payment has its own 7-day refund window from the date of that payment (after 7 days a payment is not refundable except for our technical failure). Email fastschedulebot@gmail.com with the transaction ID, your Telegram username, the purchase date and a short reason — we acknowledge within 7 business days.

My bot token was revoked by @BotFather

If you reset your bot token in @BotFather, the old token stops working immediately. Go to Bots → 🔁 Replace Token under the bot and enter the new token from @BotFather. Your scheduled messages will resume once the new token is saved.

Privacy

10 articles

The bot stores what it needs to run your schedule:
• Telegram identifiers — your user ID, and the IDs of channels you connect.
• Your content — message texts, media references, poll and button data.
• Settings — language, time zone, signature, and the bot tokens you connect (stored encrypted).

What it never does
• It does not read channels it is not connected to.
• It does not post anything you did not schedule.
• It does not sell or share your data with advertisers.

Settings & Configuration

2 articles

Settings are split between your account and each channel.

Account settings

Open ⚙️ Settings (right button on the sender bot's page 2) or send /settings:
• 🌐 Language — interface language.
• 🕐 Time Zone — the default zone.
• 💾 Media Storage — your reusable files.
• 📝 Signature — a footer for posts (Premium).
• ⭐ Premium — plan and payment.
• 🎁 Referral Program — invite link and rewards.
• 📮 Feedback — opens the support entry point.
• 📋 All commands — the full command list.

Channel settings

In the sender bot, the left ⚙️ Settings:
• 🔒 Hide channel from strangers / 🔓 Show — Public Mode.
• 👥 Admins — owner-only co-admin management.
• 🏠 Open @FastSchedulerBot — back to the hub.

Channel-level values

Time zone, signature, sender bot and admins belong to the channel, not the account. Change them from the channel card or the sender bot, not from account settings.

Commands as shortcuts

/language, /timezone, /signature, /storage, /commands open the same screens. The full list is in All Commands.
