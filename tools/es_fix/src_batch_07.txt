KEY: help.ms_copy.content
Copy places a duplicate of an item into another box while the original stays where it is — unlike Move, which relocates the item.

<b>When copying beats moving</b>
• A logo that belongs to several campaign boxes at once.
• Seasonal collections that share evergreen assets.
• A "favourites" box that mirrors items from working boxes.

Copies are independent: editing or deleting one copy never affects the other, and every schedule that references either box keeps working.

KEY: help.import_formats.content
The import flow accepts:
• <b>.fsback / .fspback</b> — full bot backups (restore with Overwrite or Add).
• <b>.txt</b> — message lists; a blank line starts a new message, with the date on its own line (see the Format topic).
• <b>.csv</b> — spreadsheets with <code>Date/Time</code> and <code>Content</code> columns.
• <b>SetDate files</b> — schedules generated by @SetDate_bot, imported as-is.
• <b>.json</b> — structured exports.

Everything except full backups imports into your connected channel, in the channel's time zone. Connect the channel first, then import.
