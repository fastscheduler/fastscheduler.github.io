KEY: help.schedule_delete.content
Remove a message you no longer need:
• From <b>Message List</b>: tap the delete/trash button on the message.
• Use <code>/delete</code> and follow the flow.
• <b>Delete by ID</b>: enter a specific job ID (e.g. <code>s_123</code>).

Deletion is <b>permanent</b> - the message will never be sent. Deleting a channel or bot only <b>pauses</b> its messages, it doesn't delete them.
