Fast Scheduler refund policy — how refunds work for Fast Scheduler Premium in @FastSchedulerBot, paid with Telegram Stars. This is the official policy for the Telegram post scheduler at https://fastscheduler.github.io/; it is not connected to any other product that shares the name.

Fast Scheduler refund policy: quick answers

Can I get a refund for Fast Scheduler Premium? Premium is paid with Telegram Stars. Refunds are discretionary and limited to 7 days from that specific payment. Email fastschedulebot@gmail.com with the transaction ID and your Telegram username.

How long do I have to request a Fast Scheduler refund? Each Stars payment has its own 7-day window counted from the charge date. After 7 days that payment is not refundable, unless Fast Scheduler failed to deliver the paid feature.

How do I cancel Fast Scheduler Premium? Open /premium in @FastSchedulerBot and cancel — see Cancel Premium. Cancelling stops the next charge; the current paid period is not refunded.

Where are the paid features described? See Premium and the in-bot checkout. Billing runs entirely through Telegram Stars.

1. Overview

This Refund Policy explains how refunds work for Premium subscriptions to Fast Scheduler ("the Service"), operated by Maxim Mkrtchyan, an individual operator based in Armenia ("we", "us"). Premium is paid exclusively with Telegram Stars (in-app virtual currency), processed entirely by Telegram.

Because Telegram Stars purchases are governed by Telegram's own policies, refunds are limited. Please read this policy before purchasing.

Brand-name clarification: Fast Support and Fast Community are names in the Fast Scheduler brand family. In these names, “Fast” is a brand element, not a promise or description that support responses, moderation, or community activity are instantaneous or faster than another service.

2. How Stars Billing Works

  1. Monthly plans renew automatically every 30 days until cancelled. Cancel anytime via the in-bot /premium menu (cancellation is additionally registered at Telegram); cancellation stops future charges but does not refund the current period.
  2. 3-month and 6-month prepaid plans are one-time purchases at a discounted rate (currently 10% and 13% off the plain monthly price respectively) and do not auto-renew.
  3. Yearly plans are one-time prepaid purchases at a discounted rate (currently 17% off twelve months at the monthly price) and do not auto-renew.
  4. Reference USD prices are listed in our Terms of Service; the in-bot prices at checkout are authoritative (plans are billed in Telegram Stars at Telegram's rate).
  5. Cooling-off / right of withdrawal (EU/UK): For digitally supplied services, the 14-day right of withdrawal generally does not apply once delivery/performance has begun with your prior explicit consent (i.e., once your Premium features are activated). Where the right does apply (e.g., you cancel before any Premium feature is used), you may be entitled to a remedy under applicable law.

3. Discretionary Refunds — 7-Day Per-Payment Window

We do not refund Telegram Stars purchases as a rule — Stars transactions are final under Telegram's policies. Outside mandatory consumer rights, we may grant a refund at our sole discretion where, for example:
- You were charged twice for the same subscription period;
- A technical fault on our side prevented you from using the Service you paid for; or
- We terminated your access without cause.

Refund window (exact rule): each Stars payment has its own 7-day refund window counted from the date of that specific payment. Eligibility is evaluated per payment, not per user or per month — for example, if month 1 was used normally and month 2 auto-renewed by accident, only the month 2 payment can be refunded (and only if it is still within 7 days of the month 2 charge). After 7 days from a payment, that payment is not refundable, even if premium was unused. Exception: a technical failure on our side (paid but premium was not activated, double charge) may be approved manually as an admin override.

Discretionary refunds are evaluated case by case, and only where Telegram's platform permits a reversal. Granting a refund in one case does not create a precedent.

How an approved refund is processed

  1. Your Premium access is revoked immediately, the refund is recorded with a timestamp, and you receive an in-bot notification.
  2. The funds return exclusively through Telegram within its processing constraints. We cannot transfer money to you directly.
  3. Refunds, where granted, cover the current paid period in full; partial (pro-rated) refunds are not offered.
  4. Referral Premium days and promo-code free periods have no monetary value and are never refundable; where Premium was granted partly through referrals, only the paid portion counts.

4. How to Request a Refund

  1. Email fastschedulebot@gmail.com with your transaction ID, Telegram username, and reason.
  2. Include the date of purchase and the email/account used where applicable.

We aim to acknowledge refund requests within 7 business days.

5. Auto-Renewal and Cancellation

  1. Stars monthly plans renew automatically until cancelled. Cancel via in-bot /premium to stop future billing. Already-paid periods are not refunded solely for cancellation.
  2. Stars 3-month, 6-month, and yearly plans are one-time purchases and do not auto-renew.
  3. Downgrading from Premium to Free takes effect at the end of the paid period.
  4. Termination for violations: if your account is terminated for Terms of Service violations, any active Premium subscription is cancelled automatically — the current paid period is not refunded, but you will not be charged again.

6. Trials and Promo Codes

  1. Any free period or welcome offer is provided at no cost; converting to a paid plan results in a charge per the prices in our Terms.
  2. Promo codes may reduce or waive a charge; they do not create an independent refund right.
  3. Where a promo resulted in no payment, no refund is applicable.

7. Chargebacks and Abuse

  1. Abuse of chargebacks (e.g., initiating a bank chargeback after receiving the Service) may result in termination of access and, where permitted, pursuit of the amount owed.
  2. No refund is granted for accounts terminated for Terms of Service violations (see Terms, Acceptable Use).

8. Processing Times

  1. Approved refunds are processed within 7 business days where technically feasible, and appear according to Telegram's own timelines.
