## Day 13 — Formatting cheatsheet (`#tips`)

💡 <b>4 rules for readable posts</b>

<b>1. Air</b> — 3–4 short lines per paragraph, no walls of text.
<b>2. Monospace</b> — wrap codes with backticks: <code>`PROMO2026`</code>.
<b>3. Spoilers</b> — <code>||hidden||</code> for quizzes and teasers.
<b>4. Spacing is safe</b> — the bot keeps your line breaks exactly as sent.
